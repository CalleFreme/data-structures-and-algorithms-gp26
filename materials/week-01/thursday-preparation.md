# Preparation for Thursday — 15–20 minutes

Before Thursday, review the short introductory material on algorithmic growth provided in Omniway/course materials.

Think about these two questions:

1. If an algorithm scans every element once, what happens to the amount of work when the amount of data doubles?
2. If every element is compared with every other element, what happens to the amount of work when the amount of data doubles?

You do **not** need to understand Big-O notation before Thursday.
